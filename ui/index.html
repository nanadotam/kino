Welcome to Kino

Kino frees up space on your Mac by clearing junk files that apps leave behind. Nothing is deleted until you click Clean.

🧹

What is “cache”?

Apps save temporary copies of pictures, web pages and songs so they open faster. Over time these can take many gigabytes. Clearing them is safe: apps make new ones when needed.

☑︎

How it works

Tick what you want to clear, then click Clean selected. Items under Be careful are never ticked for you. Some system junk needs your Mac password. macOS asks for it itself, and Kino never sees or stores it.
